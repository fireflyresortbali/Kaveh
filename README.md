# Kaveh's Banner

A playable browser prototype of a real-time strategy game in the style of Age of Mythology, set in the Shahnameh: Kaveh the blacksmith's uprising against the serpent king Zahhak.

Open `index.html` in a browser (Chrome or Safari; phone in landscape or desktop). It loads three.js from a CDN; everything else — models, textures, music and sound — is generated in code.

## Features
- 3D terrain with rivers, stone fords, mountains, trees and grass
- Jointed, animated characters: villagers (chop, mine, hoe, harvest, build), spearmen, archers, horsemen, heroes (Kaveh, Fereydun), demons, Zahhak, the Simurgh
- Economy: food, wood, gold, Favor; houses, storehouse, farms, barracks, temple, tower, stable
- Two ages, god powers, building upgrades (Masons, Fortified Town Center, Guard Tower) and economy/military upgrades
- Touch-first controls: tap to select, drag a unit to move it, drag the map to pan, pinch to zoom, rally points
- Story panels, objectives, enemy waves and a boss fight
- Procedural Persian-style music (Homayoun mode, santur, ney, daf) and sound effects

## Status
Prototype for testing gameplay and story. The planned iOS/Android version will be built in Unity with artist-made assets.
